02The four-layer stack

Agent commerce is four layers.
Privacy is the conjunction.

A deployment can be private at one layer and leaky at another. The composite property only holds if every layer holds. Most existing literature lives at one rung.

L1 (network metadata) and L4 (workload structure and content) carry the deepest threats. The taxonomy is upside-down from the conversation.

03Discourse vs threat

06Open problems

Three load-bearing gaps,
nobody is closing in the open.

Eight in the source. Three with the highest ratio of leverage to attention.

01 / 08

The proof-of-payment field in ERC-8004.

Optional in v1, expected to populate as reputation systems mature. Every reputation-bearing call carrying an on-chain receipt is the quietest, most production-ready de-anonymization in the entire stack. A ZK-validator extension is the right defense.

02 / 08

ACT-of-N: Coconut-threshold ACT.

ACT is single-issuer-and-origin. Bringing its credit-bearing semantics into Coconut-shaped t-of-n threshold issuance removes the issuer-compulsion gap. PhD-thesis-shaped piece of work. Nobody is working on it in the open.

08 / 08

Stylometric de-anonymization of inference prompts.

The single most underweighted threat. The content of the prompt is sufficient for re-identification regardless of payment scheme. Anonymous payment for inference is a partial answer until prompt-stylometric resistance ships.
